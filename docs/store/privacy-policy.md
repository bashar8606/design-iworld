collects and why.

## What the game stores on your device

Your progress, settings and purchases ledger are saved on your device. We cannot see them.

## What is sent to services we use

- **Analytics (PostHog):** which features you use (for example, buildings built, sessions and
  their length, tutorial steps), your app version, install date, and a random identifier created
  on your device. We use this to make the game better. It is not used to identify you.
- **Ads (Google AdMob):** to show ads, Google receives device and usage information. On iOS, Google
  receives the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked. You can change that
  at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the European Economic Area,
  the UK and some US states are asked for consent through Google's consent form. See Google's
  policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- **Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple):** when you buy something, Apple processes the payment.
  RevenueCat receives the purchase record and a random identifier so purchases can be restored.
- **Crash reports (Sentry):** when the game crashes, a report with technical details (device model,
  OS version, the error) is sent so we can fix it.
- **Feedback:** if you send feedback in the game, your rating and message are sent to our
  analytics.

We do not sell your data. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or
photos.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
