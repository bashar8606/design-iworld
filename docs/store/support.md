**Contact:** [email address]. We usually answer within two working days.

## Common questions

**My purchase did not arrive.** Close and reopen the game: purchases interrupted by the app
closing are delivered on the next launch. If it is still missing, open Settings → Restore
purchases.

**I reinstalled the game and lost Remove Ads or 2x income.** Open Settings → Restore purchases.
Gem packs are consumed when bought and are not restored.

**Will my progress carry over to a new phone?** Progress is saved on the device. Restore purchases
brings back Remove Ads and 2x income.

**How do offline earnings work?** Your staff keep working while you are away at half speed, for
up to 8 hours.

**How do I turn off ad tracking?** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

**Can I play without ads?** Rewarded ads are always optional. Remove Ads stops all other ads.
